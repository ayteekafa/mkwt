Where each area belongs

  • World Wide Tracker: quick 3-lap and intermission logging, VR changes, and recent matches.
  • Lounge: race-by-race mogi tracking for 12p and 24p, including intermission handling.
  • Time Trial: PB storage against current world records.
  • Combo Builder: hidden-stat totals and direct combo comparison.
  • Stats / Lounge Stats: the deeper chart and review layer, separate from fast logging.

Guest vs Account

  • Guest mode stays local in your browser and is locked to Dendo Denim.
  • Account mode stores your profile in the cloud and follows you across devices.
  • Theme choice now syncs with your account, so logging in on another device keeps your selected look.
  • Guest is still useful for trying MKWT quickly without creating an account.

What syncs now

  • Profile basics: nickname and current VR.
  • World Wide matches: your tracked match history and review data.
  • Lounge data: saved mogis, race rows, and MKCentral-linked review context.
  • Theme preference: your chosen account theme follows your next login.

What stays local

  • Guest data never leaves the browser.
  • Fresh experiments in Guest are good for quick tests without touching account history.
  • Device-specific feel like temporary cache or browser-only state can still differ until saved or synced.

FAQ

Do I need an account to use MKWT? No. Guest still works, but account mode is the better long-term option if you want sync and cleaner continuity.

Why move bigger charts out of some tracker pages? To keep the trackers faster and cleaner while putting the heavier analysis into Stats and Lounge Stats.

Does MKWT sync everything instantly? Account data and theme preference sync through the cloud once saved; Guest remains browser-local by design.

Is MKWT affiliated with Nintendo? No.
