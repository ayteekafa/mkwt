About

About MKWT

MKWT is a Mario Kart World tracker for logging matches and reviewing performance over time. If you're looking for an MK World tracker to track VR changes, sessions, and stats, MKWT keeps everything in one place.

Login / Start Open as Guest

What you can track

  • Matches: track / intermission, route, opponents, placement, and VR after.
  • VR changes: gains and losses per match, with helpful summaries.
  • Sessions: see progress blocks and how your VR moved across a set of games.
  • Stats & charts: distributions, win rates, averages, and performance by track/intermission.

Guest vs Account

  • Guest mode stores data locally in your browser (no cloud).
  • Account mode uses Supabase cloud to sync your matches across devices.
  • Some settings may be limited in Guest mode by design.

FAQ

Does MKWT require an account? No. You can try it as Guest. For syncing across devices, use an account.

Is MKWT affiliated with Nintendo? No.
